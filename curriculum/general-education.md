# General Education

UNM General Education gives students a shared academic foundation alongside the major. Gen Ed courses help students practice communication, critical thinking, information analysis, quantitative reasoning, and responsibility toward local and global communities.

The program is also exploratory. Students can use Gen Ed areas to discover fields, prepare for later coursework, and build flexible habits of inquiry across disciplines.

UNM's General Education Program is currently organized as 31 credit hours across seven areas:

| Area | Focus |
|---|---|
| Area I | Communication |
| Area II | Mathematics and Statistics |
| Area III | Physical and Natural Sciences |
| Area IV | Social and Behavioral Sciences |
| Area V-A | Humanities |
| Area V-B | Second Language |
| Area VI | Creative and Fine Arts |
| Area VII | Flexible student choice |

The official Gen Ed site maintains the current course lists, area descriptions, instructor information, essential skills, and catalog-specific requirements. Students and advisors should use the official site and the UNM Catalog for current requirements, approved courses, grading rules, and prior-catalog lists.

## Curriculum Review Notes

For curriculum proposals, Gen Ed status can affect more than a single course. Proposals involving Gen Ed courses should make clear:

- Whether the course is seeking, changing, or removing Gen Ed status.
- Which Gen Ed area or skill expectations are relevant.
- Whether the change affects degree plans, advising, transfer, or catalog language.
- Whether the proposal has implications for students under current or prior catalogs.

When in doubt, proposers should consult the official Gen Ed site and the appropriate Gen Ed review process before submitting related Kuali changes.
