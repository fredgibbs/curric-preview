# Common Course Numbering

Common Course Numbering (CCN) is New Mexico’s statewide system for lower-division academic and technical courses. It is meant to support transfer, transcript clarity, and consistency across public higher education institutions.

This page summarizes how CCN fits into UNM curriculum workflow.

---

## What CCN Does

The Common Numbering System uses a four-letter subject code and a four-digit course number. For common-numbered courses, the New Mexico Higher Education Department (NMHED) maintains statewide course information, including course titles, descriptions, and student learning outcomes.

NMHED works with statewide committees to determine subject codes, course numbers, and whether lower-division courses are comparable across institutions.

---

## Faculty Authority at UNM

Common Course Numbering does not replace faculty academic judgment. UNM faculty continue to determine course materials, teaching methods, assignments, approaches, and specific course content for CCN courses offered at UNM.

CCN is about statewide numbering and transfer alignment. It is not a template for how faculty must teach a course.

---

## When CCN Applies

CCN applies to lower-division academic and technical courses that must be integrated into the statewide numbering system.

You may need to use the NMHED CCN process when you are proposing to:

- Adopt a common-numbered course.
- Change an existing common-numbered course.
- Remove a common-numbered course.
- Request a unique lower-division course.

---

## How CCN Fits with Kuali

For lower-division course proposals affected by CCN, complete the Comprehensive NMHED CNS/CCN form and upload it to the Kuali course form.

The UNM curriculum proposal still moves through UNM’s curriculum review process. After the course has been reviewed and approved through UNM curriculum workflow, the Associate Registrar submits the CCN request to the NMHED portal and curriculum committee.

In short:

1. Prepare the UNM Kuali course proposal.
2. Complete and attach the NMHED CCN form when CCN applies.
3. Move through UNM curriculum review.
4. After UNM approval, the Associate Registrar submits the request to NMHED.

---

## What to Check Before Submitting

Before submitting a lower-division course proposal, confirm:

- The course number and level are appropriate.
- The course title and description are clear.
- The proposal explains whether the course aligns with an existing statewide course or should be treated as unique.
- Any related courses at UNM or other New Mexico institutions have been considered.
- The NMHED CCN form is attached when required.
- The Kuali proposal and CCN form are consistent.
