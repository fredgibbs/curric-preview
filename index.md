<section class="unm-intro">
  <h2 class="text-center">Welcome to Hogwarts University</h2>
  <p><img alt="UNM campus welcome scene" class="img-left" src="{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/unm/welcome.jpg">Hogwarts University is a UNM-styled mock academic site for curious students, faculty, and staff exploring a more magical version of campus life. It keeps the familiar University of New Mexico frame while trading administrative office language for castles, houses, and courses with a little more sparkle.</p>
  <p>Our faculty and house advisors support learning across charms, potions, magical history, transfiguration, student wellbeing, research, and community partnerships.</p>
  <p>The university is guided by a headmaster, a council of deans, and heads of house who help students find the right academic path from first-year orientation through advanced spellcraft.</p>
  <p>Use this preview to browse programs, curriculum resources, announcements, and campus traditions from an enchanted academic point of view.</p>
</section>
